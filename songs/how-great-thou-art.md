# How Great Thou Art

A full instrumental worship rendition of the hymn.

## Rights / lyrics note

- The **melody** is the public-domain Swedish folk tune **"O Store Gud"**
  (Carl Boberg, 1885), so it is rendered here in full.
- The famous **English lyrics** ("O Lord my God, when I in awesome wonder…")
