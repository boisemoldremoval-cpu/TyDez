# How Great Thou Art  →  Bigger Than the Storm  →  How Great Thou Art

A worship medley: the hymn opens and closes it, the posted song sits in the middle.

## Structure (what the renderer plays)

`make_storm_hymn_medley.py` — D major, ~74 BPM, ~3:56:

| Section                       | Feel                                             |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| How Great Thou Art (verse)    | Reverent, sparse fingerpicked guitar + strings   |
| Verse 1 / Verse 2             | Groove eases in — brushed drums, upright bass     |
| Chorus                        | Open, full band                                  |
