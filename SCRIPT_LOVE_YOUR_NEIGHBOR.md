# TyGuy's Bible Club — "Who Is My Neighbor?"
### Theme: Loving Your Neighbor • Verse: **Mark 12:31** • ~3 minutes

**Memory verse (on screen + spoken):** *"Love your neighbor as yourself." — Mark 12:31*
**Childlike message:** *A neighbor isn't just the kid next door — it's ANYONE who needs
a little kindness. And you can be a good neighbor today!*
**Repeating refrain (kids can say along):** *"Be a good neighbor — to everyone!"*

Host: **Kid TyGuy** (kid voice). Narrator for the Bible story (warm, gentle).

---

### 1 — Hello! (Club room) ~12s
**TyGuy (waving):** "Hey friends, it's TyGuy! Welcome back to Bible Club!"
**TyGuy:** "Today we've got a BIG question..."

### 2 — The Big Question ~12s
**TyGuy (holding a Bible):** "One day, a man asked Jesus, *'Who is my neighbor?'*"
**TyGuy:** "So Jesus told a story. Wanna hear it? Let's go!"
*(storybook pages flip → Bible times)*

### 3 — The Traveler ~12s
**Narrator:** "A man was walking down a long, lonely road..."
*(kind traveler walks the desert road, humming)*

### 4 — Trouble on the Road ~12s
**Narrator:** "But robbers came, took his things, and hurt him. They left him all alone."
*(no scary visuals — cut to the man sitting hurt at the roadside)*

### 5 — The Priest Passes ~12s
**Narrator:** "A priest came by. He saw the hurt man... and walked right past."
*(priest crosses to the far side)*

### 6 — The Helper Passes Too ~12s
**Narrator:** "Then a temple helper came by. He looked... and he kept on walking too."
*(Levite hurries past; the hurt man looks sad and alone)*

### 7 — The Good Samaritan Stops ~16s
**Narrator:** "But then a Samaritan came — someone people didn't expect to help."
**Samaritan (kneeling, kind):** "Don't worry — I've got you."
*(he bandages the man's arm gently)*

### 8 — Caring All the Way ~14s
**Narrator:** "He lifted the man onto his donkey and took him to an inn to rest..."
*(travels at dusk to a cozy inn)*

### 9 — Going the Extra Mile ~12s
**Samaritan (to innkeeper, giving coins):** "Take care of him — I'll pay whatever it costs."
**Narrator:** "He gave his own money to help a stranger."

### 10 — Who Was the Neighbor? ~16s
*(back in the club room)*
**TyGuy:** "So... who was the REAL neighbor? Not the fancy ones who walked by..."
**TyGuy (big smile):** "...it was the one who STOPPED to help! Jesus said, 'Go and do the same.'"

### 11 — The Verse ~14s
*(bright card, gentle music, glowing cross)*
**TyGuy (warm):** "The Bible says: *'Love your neighbor as yourself.'* — Mark 12, verse 31."

### 12 — Be a Good Neighbor Today ~22s
**Narrator (cheerful, over kids being kind):** "A neighbor is anyone who needs you!"
- A kid helps another pick up dropped books.
- A kid shares lunch with someone who has none.
- A kid invites a lonely new kid to play.
**Kids (together):** "Be a good neighbor — to everyone!"

### 13 — Closing ~12s
**TyGuy (thumbs-up):** "This week, find one way to be a good neighbor. You can do it!"
**TyGuy:** "Be kind, have fun, and be you! See you next time at Bible Club!"

---

**Total ≈ 3:00.** Wraps with the series outro bumper.
*Production note: kids are shown but only TyGuy + the narrator speak (clean kid voice via TTS).
Verse + refrain appear on screen for little ones to read along.*
