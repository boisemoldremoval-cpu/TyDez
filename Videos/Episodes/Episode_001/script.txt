 EPISODE 001 SCRIPT — "Taking Turns"
==============================================================================

BIBLE VERSE
Ephesians 4:32 — "Be kind to one another, tender-hearted, forgiving one
another, as God in Christ forgave you."

LIFE LESSON
When someone is unfair to us, Jesus helps us choose kindness and forgiveness
instead of anger.

LOGLINE
Kelliee's turn on the swing gets taken, and she wants to shout — until TyGuy
shows her what God's Word says about kindness and forgiveness, and the
playground becomes peaceful again.

CALL TO FOLLOW JESUS
Jesus forgives us, so we can forgive others. Ask Him into your heart and let
Him help you be kind every day.

Runtime target: ~45s across 7 clips   |   Aspect: 16:9
